# BumpOne.lol — Future Expansion

Do not build these into MVP unless validated.

---

## Future Capabilities Under Consideration

* more categories
* multiple boards
* historical boards
* public profile analytics
* richer profile pages
* advanced sharing
* notifications
* additional social interactions

Do NOT add additional payment mechanics until the core ranking/payment system has been proven stable.

---

## Notifications

Notify users when:

- they are bumped
- they leave top 100
- they return to top 100
- someone enters immediately above them
- they receive a new reaction
- someone shares their profile

---

## Historical Boards

Daily/weekly snapshots of the wall.

Show how the wall looked at a specific point in time.

---

## Multiple Boards

Potential future model:

BumpOne.lol

---

## Paid Enhancements

Potential future products:

- animated profile
- verified profile
- custom effects
- profile statistics
- featured links
- custom reactions

These should not interfere with the core ranking fairness unless explicitly designed into the rules.

---

## Advanced Analytics Dashboard

Show users:

- their rank history over time
- comparison with other profiles
- reaction trends
- share performance

---

## API Access

Public API for:

- reading board state
- reading profile data
- webhook integrations

---

## Important

The MVP should prove one thing:

> Will people pay to climb the board?

Everything else comes later.
