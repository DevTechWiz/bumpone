# BumpOne.lol — Project Overview

## Product: The Internet's Live Attention Market

BumpOne.lol is a living visual arena and real-time marketplace for internet attention. Profiles, startups, creators, and brands compete for digital turf, physically bump each other down the wall, build an immutable ranking history, and capture live visual exposure.

The core concept is:

> **Claim your turf. Bump everyone below you. Rule the internet's live attention board.**

---

## Product Positioning: Attention Market, Not Just a Leaderboard

BumpOne.lol is fundamentally differentiated from simple static paid leaderboards:

1. **Measurable Attention & Real Estate**: Buyers aren't just paying for an abstract rank number—they are buying **physical visual prominence** (a massive 4×4 King center tile, 2×2 Elite tiles, profile views, outbound clicks, and viral share moments).
2. **"Bump Battles" (Player vs. Player)**: Taking #1 isn't a silent checkout—it's a broadcasted battle event (*"Alex stormed the Throne and displaced 14 profiles"*).
3. **The Concentric Top 100 & The Graveyard**:
   * **#1 The King Throne**: A colossal 4×4 center citadel dominating the arena.
   * **#2–#13 The Inner Orbit Elites**: 2×2 prominent display tiles.
   * **#14–#40 The Vanguard**: Enhanced 1×1 tiles.
   * **#41–#100 The Contenders**: 1×1 tiles on the outer perimeter.
   * **#101+ The Graveyard**: Profiles knocked off the board fall into the Graveyard Drawer, where they can reclaim their turf at any time.
4. **Three Discovery Signals**:
   * 💰 **Power**: Authoritative ranking by Active Value (dollars paid).
   * ❤️ **Popular**: Community-ranked by anonymous emoji reactions (🔥, 👀, ❤️, 😂).
   * 📈 **Trending**: Momentum ranking by climb velocity and recent bump activity.
5. **Prestige & Lifetime Spend Transparency**:
   * Profiles display both their **Active Value** (current ranking strength) and **Total Lifetime Spend** (e.g. *"Total Spent: $1,200 | Active Value: $710"*).

## Core Product Loop

```text
Discover live board
   ↓
Identify target slot / throne ("I can overtake #1")
   ↓
Pay top-up via Dodo Hosted Checkout
   ↓
BUMP! Live sound FX + visual board rearrangement
   ↓
Broadcast to live feed & War Room
   ↓
Viral share card generated for X / social media
   ↓
Displaced competitor gets notified / returns to defend
```

## Product Philosophy

BumpOne.lol feels like:
* A live internet spectacle & battleground
* A game of status, ego, and high-visibility marketing
* An honest, transparent attention market where money talks and player-vs-player competition rules
* A viral launchpad for builders, creators, and companies

It does NOT feel like:
* A boring directory
* A predatory timer-decay game (buyers own their active value permanently until an actual human outbids them)
* An opaque ad network with hidden algorithms
