# Carry-Forward, explained: what it costs to storm the Throne

The stake you put on the board is capital that follows you.

## The mechanic

On BumpOne.lol, every profile has **active value** - the dollars you've
staked, still standing. Rank is active value, sorted descending. #1 has
more than #2, always.

When you want to move up, you don't buy a new position. You pay the
difference between what you hold and what your target holds, plus a $10
minimum top-up. The money you've already put in keeps working for you.

## The worked example (numbers are the rulebook, not marketing)

Say the board looks like this:

- #1 holds $700
- #50 is you, holding $500

You want the Throne (the colossal center tile that marks #1 on the board).
What do you pay?

**$700 − $500 + $10 = $210.**

Your active value becomes $500 + $210 = $710. That's not $710 paid for the
Throne - $500 of it is the stake you already had. You just topped up
$210 to be strictly above the King, because a takeover must *exceed* the
target - there are no ties, no photo finishes, no permanent rank ceiling.

Now every profile below you computes the same equation about *you*.
And the displaced King gets the notification.

## Why the design matters

Two properties fall out of this, straight from the product rules:

1. **Climbing is cumulative.** Nobody wipes your progress. Fall to the
   Graveyard (the analog billboard below the visible 100), come back, and
   your next top-up still builds on what you've spent before - the market
   keeps the score.
2. **The gap is always a number.** To take a position, you can see exactly
   what it costs, because spends are public. Profiles show both active
   value and lifetime spend. There is no hidden buy-in, no mystery pricing.

That second rule is the honest part of an attention market. A competitor
can't be secretly richer than they look. Their spend is on the profile,
right next to their current strength.

## The smallest legal move: $10

Entry is $10 minimum. That won't take a Throne - but it takes a tile of
your own on the outer ring, and the ranking doesn't stop at 100.
