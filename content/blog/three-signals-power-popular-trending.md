# Three signals: how Power, Popular and Trending sort the board

A board that sorts one way sorts nobody. BumpOne.lol runs **three
discovery signals at once** on the same arena, because attention isn't one
dimension - and the design never pretends it is.

## Power - the market's spine

Power is the authoritative ranking: straight by active value, dollars
staked, descending. This is the board's ground truth. #1 in Power owns the
King Throne - the massive center tile that every takeover drama resolves
around. If you want the game's scoreboard, this is it. Dollars on the
table, sorted, visible.

## Popular - the crowd's own vote

Popular re-ranks profiles by emoji reactions. A profile the crowd loves
can ride far above profiles with bigger budgets on this signal - and that
shows up honestly as a separate ranking, not as a "score" blended into
everything else. Money sorts one list; affection sorts another; both are
forwarded to everyone looking at the wall.

## Trending - who's moving right now

Trending is momentum - climb velocity and recent bump activity. The profile
that is #60 today and #18 by the weekend: trending is how that run
gets spotted, early, while it's still a run. Fast risers surface on a
dedicated ranking of their own instead of being invisible between the
static tiers.

## Why three lists beat one score

Because each signal answers a different question a visitor might actually
be asking:

- "Who's the strongest?" → Power
- "Who does the crowd actually like?" → Popular
- "Who's worth watching this week?" → Trending

The mechanics stay market-honest throughout. Spends are public on every
profile (active value today, lifetime spend across the account), so none
of these rankings can be gamed into looking richer or bigger than the
board's own data says. It's a paid attention market that shows its own
ledger - power from real money, popularity from real people, momentum
from real movement.
